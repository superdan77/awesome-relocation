# Awesome Relocation [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of practical resources for tech workers moving to another country: job boards, sponsor lists, housing, banking, tax, healthcare and country guides.

This list is about the move itself, not about visa rules. Every entry links to the official site or the canonical repository.

## Visa-Sponsored Job Boards

- [Arbeitnow](https://arbeitnow.com) - German job board with a filter for roles that offer visa sponsorship.
- [EURES](https://europa.eu/eures/portal/jv-se/home) - European Commission job mobility portal covering the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland.
- [EuroTechJobs](https://www.eurotechjobs.com) - Technology jobs across Europe, with a relocation package filter.
- [GaijinPot Jobs](https://www.gaijinpot.com) - Jobs in Japan aimed at foreign residents and applicants from abroad.
- [German Tech Jobs](https://www.germantechjobs.de) - Developer roles in Germany with salary ranges and a visa support filter.
- [Japan Dev](https://japan-dev.com) - Curated software jobs at Japanese companies that hire internationally.
- [Landing.jobs](https://landing.jobs) - Tech jobs in Portugal and the rest of Europe, with relocation and remote filters.
- [Make it in Germany Job Listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/jobs/job-listings) - Federal government job search for skilled workers from outside the EU.
- [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me) - Curated tech jobs where the employer pays for relocation, with visa and family details on each listing.
- [SwissDevJobs](https://www.swissdevjobs.ch) - Developer jobs in Switzerland with published salary ranges.
- [TokyoDev](https://tokyodev.com) - Software jobs in Japan for English speakers, with visa sponsorship marked per listing.
- [USponsorMe](https://usponsorme.com) - Search over jobs at employers with a history of sponsoring US work visas.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com) - Startup job marketplace with a filter for companies that sponsor visas.
- [Work in Denmark](https://www.workindenmark.dk) - Danish public employment service for international candidates.
- [Work in Estonia](https://workinestonia.com) - Government talent portal with job listings and relocation guides.
- [Work in Finland](https://www.workinfinland.com/en/) - Official portal listing Finnish employers hiring international professionals.

## Sponsoring Company Lists

- [H-1B Employer Data Hub](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/h-1b-employer-data-hub) - USCIS data on which US employers filed H-1B petitions, by year, state and industry.
- [H1BData.info](https://h1bdata.info) - Searchable database of H-1B salary disclosure filings by employer, job title and city.
- [MyVisaJobs](https://www.myvisajobs.com) - Rankings and filings data for US employers that sponsor H-1B visas and green cards.
- [OFLC Performance Data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) - Raw US Department of Labor disclosure files for every labour certification programme.
- [Public register of recognised sponsors](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors) - Official Dutch list of employers allowed to sponsor highly skilled migrants.
- [Register of licensed sponsors](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers) - Official UK list of employers licensed to sponsor Skilled Worker and other work visas.

- [Deel](https://www.deel.com) - Employer of record and payroll platform with a visa and immigration service.

- [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com) - Relocation and immigration case management focused on Europe.

- [Oyster](https://www.oysterhr.com) - Employer of record with hiring, payroll and relocation support.
- [Remote](https://remote.com) - Employer of record, payroll and contractor platform with a visa service.
- [Velocity Global](https://www.velocityglobal.com) - Employer of record with immigration and global mobility services.

## Cost of Living and Salary Tools

- [Expatistan](https://www.expatistan.com) - Cost of living comparison between two cities, built from crowdsourced prices.
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com) - Salary reports, company reviews and interview accounts by employer and location.
- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) - Verified compensation data for tech roles, broken down by level, company and city.
- [LivingCost.org](https://livingcost.org) - Cost of living estimates for cities and countries with a single-person budget breakdown.
- [Mercer Cost of Living](https://www.mercer.com/insights/total-rewards/talent-mobility-insights/cost-of-living/) - Annual ranking of the most expensive cities for international assignees.
- [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com) - Crowdsourced prices, rent, quality of life and crime indices for cities worldwide.
- [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com) - Salary data and a cost of living calculator for comparing two cities.

## Tax and Social Security

- [Bright!Tax](https://bright.tax) - Tax preparation service for US citizens living outside the United States.
- [EU social security coordination](https://ec.europa.eu/social/main.jsp?catId=849&langId=en) - European Commission rules on which country you pay contributions in when you move.
- [Expatfile](https://www.expatfile.tax) - Self-service software for filing US expat tax returns and FBAR reports.
- [Foreign Earned Income Exclusion](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-earned-income-exclusion) - IRS rules on excluding foreign wages from US taxable income.
- [National Insurance](https://www.gov.uk/national-insurance) - UK contributions system, including how to apply for a National Insurance number.
- [Skatteverket in English](https://www.skatteverket.se/servicelankar/otherlanguages/inenglish.4.12815e4f14a62bc048f4edc.html) - Swedish Tax Agency guidance on registration, personal identity numbers and tax.
- [Social security agreements](https://www.ssa.gov/international/agreements_overview.html) - US Social Security Administration list of totalization agreements that prevent double contributions.
- [Tax on foreign income](https://www.gov.uk/tax-foreign-income/residence) - UK rules on tax residence, the statutory residence test and split-year treatment.
- [Taxes for Expats](https://www.taxesforexpats.com) - US expat tax preparation with published pricing and country guides.
- [Taxing Wages](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2026/04/taxing-wages-2026_d1f39986.html) - OECD comparison of the tax wedge on labour income across member countries.
- [Worldwide Tax Summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com) - PwC summaries of personal and corporate tax rules for over 150 territories.

## Housing

- [Blueground](https://www.blueground.com) - Furnished apartments rented by the month in cities worldwide.
- [Funda](https://www.funda.nl) - Main Dutch property portal for rentals and sales, in Dutch.
- [HousingAnywhere](https://www.housinganywhere.com) - Mid-term rentals that can be booked from abroad before you arrive.
- [Idealista](https://www.idealista.com) - Leading property portal for Spain, Italy and Portugal.
- [ImmoScout24](https://www.immobilienscout24.de) - Largest German property portal for rentals and purchases, in German.
- [Pararius](https://www.pararius.com) - Dutch rental portal with an English interface and no agency fee for tenants.
- [Rightmove](https://www.rightmove.co.uk) - Largest UK property portal for rentals and sales.
- [Spotahome](https://www.spotahome.com) - Rentals booked online after a video viewing, aimed at people moving from abroad.
- [WG-Gesucht](https://www.wg-gesucht.de) - Main German site for shared flats and sublets, with an English interface.
- [Wunderflats](https://www.wunderflats.com) - Furnished mid-term rentals in Germany that come with a registration-ready contract.
- [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com) - Main US portal for rentals and home purchases.

## Banking and Money Transfer

- [bunq](https://www.bunq.com) - European mobile bank that opens accounts for residents of the EEA online.
- [HSBC Expat](https://www.expat.hsbc.com) - Offshore multi-currency accounts aimed at people who move between countries.
- [Monito](https://www.monito.com) - Comparison engine for the real cost of an international money transfer.
- [Monzo](https://monzo.com) - UK mobile bank that accepts newly arrived residents without a long credit history.
- [N26](https://n26.com) - Mobile bank offering euro accounts across the EEA with online onboarding.
- [Nova Credit](https://www.novacredit.com) - Translates a foreign credit history into a score US lenders can use.
- [Remitly](https://www.remitly.com) - Remittance service focused on transfers to family in other countries.
- [Revolut](https://revolut.com) - Multi-currency account and card with interbank exchange rates within limits.
- [Starling Bank](https://www.starlingbank.com) - UK bank with online onboarding and no fees on foreign card spending.
- [Wise](https://wise.com) - Multi-currency account with local bank details in several countries and mid-market rate transfers.
- [Zolve](https://www.zolve.com) - Bank accounts and credit cards for newcomers to the United States with no local credit file.

## Healthcare and Insurance

- [Aetna International](https://www.aetnainternational.com) - International private medical insurance for individuals and employers.
- [Allianz Care](https://www.allianzcare.com) - Global health insurance plans for people living outside their home country.
- [Feather](https://www.feather-insurance.com) - English-language insurance broker for Germany, covering health, liability and more.
- [Genki](https://www.genki.world) - Monthly health insurance for long-stay travellers and people between countries.
- [Moving to England](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/visiting-or-moving-to-england/) - NHS guidance on who can register with a GP and who pays the immigration health surcharge.
- [ottonova](https://www.ottonova.de/en) - German private health insurer with an English-language application and app.
- [SafetyWing](https://www.safetywing.com) - Subscription travel medical insurance for remote workers and people on the move.
- [Techniker Krankenkasse](https://www.tk.de/en) - Large German public health insurer with English-language guidance for new arrivals.
- [Your Europe: healthcare](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/health/index_en.htm) - EU guide to healthcare entitlements when living or working in another member state.

## Shipping and Pets

- [Eurosender](https://www.eurosender.com) - Booking platform for parcel and pallet shipping across Europe.
- [IPATA](https://www.ipata.org) - Trade body for pet shippers, with a directory of member companies by country.
- [Pet travel](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/pet-travel) - USDA guidance on exporting a pet from the United States and importing one into it.
- [PetRelocation](https://www.petrelocation.com) - Door-to-door pet moving service handling flights, paperwork and quarantine.
- [Seven Seas Worldwide](https://www.sevenseasworldwide.com) - Shared container and excess baggage shipping for personal effects.
- [Shiply](https://www.shiply.com) - Marketplace where transport companies bid on your shipment, including vehicles.
- [Taking your pet abroad](https://www.gov.uk/take-pet-abroad) - UK rules on animal health certificates, microchipping and rabies vaccination.
- [UPakWeShip](https://www.upakweship.com) - Fixed-price container and crate shipping for self-packed household goods.

## Language and Integration

- [Alliance Française](https://www.alliancefrancaise.org) - Network of French language centres with courses and official exams.
- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) - Open source spaced repetition flashcard software, widely used for vocabulary.
- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) - Free language learning app covering most European and several Asian languages.
- [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/en/index.html) - German cultural institute offering courses and the exams accepted for residence applications.
- [Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es) - Spanish cultural institute running courses and the DELE Spanish exams.
- [italki](https://www.italki.com) - Marketplace for one-to-one lessons with language teachers and tutors.
- [OFII](https://www.ofii.fr) - French Office for Immigration and Integration, which runs the mandatory integration contract.
- [Österreichischer Integrationsfonds](https://www.integrationsfonds.at) - Austrian integration fund providing German courses and orientation for new residents.

## Communities

- [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com) - Anonymous forum for verified employees, with active offer and relocation threads.
- [Expat.com](https://www.expat.com) - Country forums, classifieds and guides for people living outside their home country.
- [InterNations](https://www.internations.org) - Membership network with local events for people who have moved abroad.
- [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com) - Local groups and events, including newcomer, language exchange and tech meetups.
- [Nomad List](https://nomadlist.com) - City data and a paid community for people choosing where to live and work.
- [r/expats](https://www.reddit.com/r/expats/) - Subreddit about living abroad, with practical threads on settling in.
- [r/IWantOut](https://www.reddit.com/r/IWantOut/) - Subreddit where people plan moves between specific countries and get feedback.
- [The Local](https://www.thelocal.com) - English-language news and practical guides for several European countries.

## Country Guides

- [Access NL](https://www.access-nl.org) - Volunteer-run guide to daily life, schooling and healthcare in the Netherlands.
- [All About Berlin](https://allaboutberlin.com) - Detailed guides to registration, visas, taxes and rentals in Berlin.
- [ch.ch](https://www.ch.ch/en/) - Official Swiss federal information portal for residents.
- [Citizens Information](https://www.citizensinformation.ie) - Irish public service explaining rights, benefits, housing and healthcare.
- [Foreign Residents Support Portal](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/support/portal/index.html) - Japanese government portal for foreign residents, with multilingual life information.
- [Handbook Germany](https://handbookgermany.de/en) - Government-funded guide to work, housing, health and paperwork in Germany.
- [IamExpat Netherlands](https://www.iamexpat.nl) - News, housing listings and practical guides for internationals in the Netherlands.
- [InfoFinland](https://www.infofinland.fi/en/) - Official multilingual guide to living, working and studying in Finland.
- [Information Sverige](https://www.informationsverige.se/en/) - Swedish government portal explaining society, work and services for new residents.
- [Life in Denmark](https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/) - Official Danish portal covering registration, tax, health and everyday services.
- [New in Norway](https://www.nyinorge.no/en/) - Official Norwegian guide to registration, tax cards and public services.
- [New Zealand Now](https://www.newzealandnow.govt.nz) - Government guide to working, living and settling in New Zealand.
- [Settle in Estonia](https://settleinestonia.ee) - Free government adaptation programme with courses for new residents.
- [Settle in Poland](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/settle-in-poland) - Polish government page on residence formalities, work and daily life.
- [Settling in Australia](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/settling-in-australia) - Department of Home Affairs guidance on the first steps after arriving.
- [Settling in Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/settle-canada.html) - Government checklist covering social insurance numbers, health cards, schools and banking.
